Account deletion

Delete your GoGatherNow account

You can request deletion of your GoGatherNow account from inside the app or by contacting support.

Delete from the app

  1. Open GoGatherNow.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Open account deletion or account settings.
  4. Submit the deletion request.

Delete by email

Email admin@gogathernow.com from the email address connected to your account. Use the subject “Delete my GoGatherNow account”.

What gets deleted

We delete or anonymize account profile data and personal identifiers where deletion is technically and legally possible. Some records may be retained for security, abuse prevention, audit, or legal compliance. Content shared with other users may remain in limited form if needed to preserve squad, activity, safety, or moderation records.
